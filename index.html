About the System

Purpose

This prototype supports our Science, Technology, and Society (STS) project, “A Proposed Barangay Community Concern and Request Management System for Tayug, Pangasinan.” It demonstrates how technology could make it easier to report community problems and follow their progress.

Intended Users

  • Residents who want to report concerns, request assistance, track progress, and read announcements.
  • Barangay officials who would review reports, update their status, and post announcements.

How It Aims to Help

When concerns arrive through many different channels, they can be hard to organize and follow up. A single system could give every report a reference number, a clear status, and a visible history, which may improve communication between residents and barangay officials and help the community see which concerns are being handled.

Privacy Notice

  • Provide only the information that is necessary. Do not enter real names, addresses, or contact numbers in this demo.
  • This is a demonstration prototype and is not an official reporting channel.
  • Data is saved with your browser's localStorage. It stays on this device and is not a secure database.
  • The administrator login is a demo only. It can be bypassed and does not provide real security.
  • This prototype is not connected to any barangay official or government system.
  • A real deployment would need a secure backend, proper authentication and authorization, data protection, and compliance with applicable laws such as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).
